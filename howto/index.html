01Base, pack & total weight

Nearly every argument about pack weight comes down to people measuring different things. Baseweight tracks three numbers, and the difference between them is the whole point.

Base weight everything except worn items and consumables
Pack weight base + consumables — what's on your back leaving the trailhead
Total weight pack + what you're wearing — every gram you're moving

Base weight is the one people quote, because it's the only one that doesn't change during a trip. Your food and water shrink as you walk; your tent doesn't. It's the honest measure of your kit choices.

Why this matters A 7 kg base weight and a 7 kg pack weight are wildly different loads. If someone quotes a number without saying which one it is, it's meaningless.

02Start your first list

When you first open Baseweight you get a starter list called My Gear List, already set up with the usual categories, plus an Example: Cape Wrath Trail list you can poke around in to see what a finished list looks like.

  1. Tap the list card on the home screen to open it.
  2. Tap + Add item under any category.
  3. Type the name, weight and quantity, then Save item.

To rename a list, add another, duplicate or delete one, open Your lists from the home screen. Keep separate lists for separate trips — summer overnighter, winter week, bikepacking — and duplicate an existing one as a starting point rather than building from scratch each time.

Categories

Categories are yours to shape. Rename them, recolour them, add your own, and drag the handle on the left to reorder both categories and the items inside them.

03Adding gear

Start typing an item name and Baseweight searches a catalogue of 518 real products — tents, quilts, packs, stoves, shoes. Pick one and the weight fills itself in. Type durs and you'll get the Durston shelters; type sawyer and you'll get the filters.

On a computer the name field is focused the moment you hit + Add item, so you can start typing straight away without reaching for the mouse.

Catalogue weights are manufacturer specs They're a starting point, not gospel. Real-world weights vary, and your scales always win — every catalogue weight is editable the moment you add it. Three conventions worth knowing:
  • Shelters are minimum trail weight — inner, fly and poles, no stakes or stuff sacks. Add your stakes as their own item.
  • Clothing is labelled with the cut and size it was weighed in, e.g. (men's M) or (women's S). Neither is "the default".
  • Footwear is a pair, e.g. (pair, men's US 9).

Deleting an item

On a phone, swipe an item left to reveal the delete button. On a computer, hover over the row and a bin icon appears on the right. You can also open any item and use Delete item.

04Worn & consumable

These two tags are what make your base weight correct, and they're the part people most often get wrong. Open any item to toggle them.

  • Worn — you're wearing or carrying it on your body, not in your pack. Shoes, shirt, shorts, cap, watch, trekking poles in your hands.
  • Consumable — you'll use it up. Food, water, fuel, sunscreen, toilet paper.

Both are excluded from base weight. Worn items are also excluded from pack weight, because they aren't on your back.

Rule of thumb If you'd still be carrying it on day five, it belongs in base weight. If you'll have eaten, drunk or burned it, it's consumable. If it never goes in the pack, it's worn.

05The gear locker

The locker is your own gear cupboard. Weigh something once, save it, and drop it into any future list without typing it again — with your real measured weight, not a spec sheet.

  1. Open an item and tap Saved to locker, or add gear directly from Gear locker on the home screen.
  2. In any list, use + Add item and your locker entries appear in the type-ahead alongside the catalogue.
  3. Or open the locker and add several items at once, choosing quantities as you go.

This is the fastest way to build a second list: put your kit in the locker once, then assemble trip lists from it in a couple of minutes.

06Reading the breakdown

The Breakdown tab is the reason Baseweight exists. A total tells you nothing actionable; a breakdown tells you what to fix.

  • The donut — your base weight split by category. Click or tap a slice to isolate it. On a computer the list below jumps to that category and opens it.
  • Ranked categories — heaviest first, with percentages. Expand any row to see the items inside it.
  • Heaviest items — your top ten single items. This is usually where the easy wins hide.
  • Pack vs bodyweight — add your bodyweight in Settings and you'll see your pack as a percentage of it, against the rough 20% guideline.

On a wide screen the breakdown sits permanently beside your gear list, so the numbers move as you edit. That live feedback is the fastest way to find weight — change a tent, watch the donut shift.

Where to actually look The "big three" — shelter, sleep system and pack — are usually 50–60% of base weight between them. If one of those isn't in your top three categories, something unusual is going on and it's worth a look.

07Packing checklist

The Checklist tab turns the same list into a tick-off sheet for packing night. Tap items as they go in the pack and watch the progress bar fill. Use Reset before your next trip — it clears the ticks, not the gear.

08Install it on your phone

Baseweight is a web app, so there's nothing to download from an app store. Add it to your home screen and it behaves like a normal app — its own icon, full screen, no browser bars, and it works offline, which matters at a trailhead with no signal.

You're already running it as an app ✓ Baseweight is open in standalone mode right now, so it's installed on this device. Nothing to do.
You'll need Safari for this On iPhone and iPad, only Safari can add an app to the home screen — Chrome, Firefox and Edge on iOS can't, no matter what their menus suggest. Open Baseweight in Safari first, then follow the steps below.
  1. Open Baseweight in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow coming out of it, at the bottom of the screen (or top right on iPad).
  3. Scroll down the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top right.

The Baseweight icon appears on your home screen and opens full screen from then on.

  1. Open Baseweight in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app — the wording varies by Android version.
  4. Confirm with Install.

Chrome often shows an "Install app" prompt on its own after a couple of visits.

  1. Open Baseweight in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look for the install icon in the address bar — a monitor with a downward arrow.
  3. Click it, then Install.

No install icon? Use the ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Install page as app. Safari on macOS: File → Add to Dock.

09Sharing your list

Open Share & transfer from the home screen. There are three ways to share.

A read-only link

Copy shareable breakdown link gives you a URL containing a snapshot of your list. Anyone who opens it sees your gear and a full interactive breakdown, read-only — they can't change anything, and it doesn't touch their own lists.

The whole list is encoded inside the link itself, so nothing is uploaded and there's no account involved. Whoever opens it can also tap Add this list to my Baseweight to keep their own editable copy — handy for sharing a kit list someone wants to adapt, and the easiest way to move a list between your own devices. It's a frozen snapshot: edit your list afterwards and the old link still shows the old version. Send a fresh link when you want to share an update.

Your bodyweight is opt-in Bodyweight is only included if you leave the toggle on in Settings. It can't be partially hidden — pack weight is visible, so any ratio would give it away — which is why it's a single on/off choice. Prices are never shared.

An image

Share as image renders your gear list, and Share breakdown report as image renders the charts — both as clean images for Instagram, a forum post or a video.

CSV

Export … as CSV gives you a spreadsheet of every item. Useful for your own analysis, or for moving a list somewhere else.

10Backup, restore & CSV

Open Backup & restore from the home screen.

  • Back up everything downloads a single file containing every list, your gear locker and your settings. Keep it somewhere safe — cloud storage, or email it to yourself.
  • Restore from a backup loads that file back. Use it to move everything to a new phone or laptop, or to undo a mess.
  • Import a CSV as new list brings in a list from a spreadsheet — handy if you're coming from LighterPack or a gear spreadsheet of your own.
Please do this before a big trip Restoring replaces what's currently there. And because your data lives only in this browser (see below), a backup is the only copy that survives a lost phone or a cleared cache.

11Moving a list between phone and laptop

Baseweight has no accounts, so nothing syncs on its own — a list you build on your phone won't turn up on your laptop. You move it across yourself, which takes under a minute. There are three ways; the first needs no file at all.

Send yourself a link — quickest for one list

  1. Open the list, then Share & transfer → Copy shareable breakdown link.
  2. Send the link to yourself however you like — email, Messages, WhatsApp, a note.
  3. Open it on the other device and tap Add this list to my Baseweight.

It arrives as a new list, so nothing you already have is touched. Categories, colours, quantities, weights and the worn and consumable tags all come across. The one thing links deliberately leave out is prices — they're never included in a share link — so those come in blank. If you track prices, use one of the file methods below instead.

Moving everything — best for a new phone or laptop

  1. On the device that has your lists: Backup & restore → Back up everything. That's one file containing every list, your gear locker and your settings.
  2. Get the file onto the other device — see below.
  3. On the other device: Backup & restore → Restore from a backup → pick the file → confirm Replace everything.
Restoring replaces — it doesn't merge Whatever is already in Baseweight on that device gets overwritten by the backup. That's exactly right when you're setting up a new phone. If the other device has lists you want to keep, use the single-list method below instead.

Moving one list — best when both devices are in use

  1. Open the list you want to move, then Share & transfer → Export … as CSV.
  2. Get the file onto the other device.
  3. There: Share & transfer → Import a CSV as new list.

This adds a list instead of replacing anything, so it's the safe option when you're using both devices. Categories, quantities, weights, units, prices and both the worn and consumable tags all survive the round trip. The one thing that doesn't is starred items — stars come back cleared, so re-star anything you rely on.

Getting the file across

  • iPhone ↔ Mac — AirDrop is quickest.
  • Anything ↔ anything — email it to yourself and open the attachment on the other device.
  • Cloud storage — save it to iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox on one device, then open it from the other.
  • Android ↔ PC — a USB cable, or Google Drive.
Links are still read-only until you choose to add Opening a share link only ever shows you the list. Nothing touches your own data unless you tap Add this list to my Baseweight and confirm — and even then it's added alongside your lists, never over the top of them.
Editing on both devices? There's no merging, so pick one device as the master and copy in one direction. If you edit the same list in both places, whichever file you import last wins and the other set of edits is gone. Accounts and proper sync are on the roadmap.

12Where your data lives

Baseweight has no accounts and no server. Your lists are stored in your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is tracked, and nobody — including me — can see your gear.

That's good for privacy, and it has two consequences worth understanding:

  • Your lists don't follow you between devices. A list made on your phone won't appear on your laptop. Use a backup file to move it across.
  • Clearing your browser data deletes your lists. "Clear browsing data", "Remove website data", or uninstalling the home-screen app can all wipe it, with no way back.
So: back up occasionally It takes five seconds and it's the only safety net there is. Accounts and sync are on the roadmap — until then, the backup file is your insurance.

13Common questions

Can I switch between grams and ounces?

Yes — Settings → units. It converts everything you've already entered, so switch whenever you like. You can also enter an individual item in a different unit from the one your list displays.

Does it cost anything?

Baseweight is free, with no ads and no accounts.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once you've opened it once — and properly so if you've added it to your home screen. Everything runs on your device.

Why is my base weight lower than I expected?

Almost always because something is tagged worn or consumable that shouldn't be — or the other way round. Open the Breakdown tab and expand the categories; tagged items are marked, so mistakes are easy to spot.

Can I have more than one list?

As many as you like. Your lists → New list, or duplicate an existing one and edit it down.

I opened my share link and got a 404

Share links point at the viewer on this site. If you saved a link from a very early version, generate a fresh one from Share & transfer.

Something's broken or missing

Baseweight is built and maintained by one person. If you hit a bug or want a feature, the quickest way to reach me is a comment on the YouTube channel.
